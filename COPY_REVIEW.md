## Deliberate deviations from the briefs

- The developers brief suggested «Für Profis» as a shared menu item once the navigation gets crowded. With seven items the navigation still fits at 1024 px and above; on smaller screens the full-screen menu lists all items. Revisit if a page is added.
- The developer segments use group-project photographs (smzh Real Estate Advisory) with a visible credit. They illustrate project types; they are not presented as SARTO interiors.
- The six service areas keep their English titles (Interior Architecture & Design, Bespoke Manufacturing …) in the German edition, as proposed in the positioning note. Descriptions are German.
- No capacity figures, references, price advantages or date guarantees on the developer page. The process note states this explicitly.
